# [WIP] Mission Impenguinable



In this top-down adventure game Commander Skitter and Krivate got abducted by Professor Blowkus! Pico and Rowalski set out on a mission to save them. As they traverse the game world they solve puzzles that let them retrace their friend’s steps. Finally, they reach Professor Blowkus’s lab, and they find their friends locked in a cage in lab room 110. They have to solve a final puzzle and defeat Professor Blowkus!

### 🎮 Controls **[REQUIRES REVISION]**
Move Left : A/Left Arrow
Move Right : D/Right Arrow
Move Up : W/Up Arrow
Move Down : S/Down Arrow
Slide : Space

### 📸 Camera Behavior **[REQUIRES REVISION]**
The camera is locked to the player character, keeping them center screen with the exception that should the player character be too close to outside the map, the camera will stop on the border to prevent the player from observing things outside of the map.

### ⚙️ Running Instructions
**Play Online**: [LIVE DEMO](https://glovolivia.github.io/-WIP-Mission-Impenguinable/)

**Play locally**
- Download and extract project files
- Open Terminal and navigate to project directory
- Run: python3 -m http.server 8000
- Open http://localhost:8000 in your browser.
